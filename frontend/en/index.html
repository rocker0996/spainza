Digital Nomad · FAQ

Questions about the 2026 residence route

These general answers do not replace an assessment of your facts and current documents.

How can I obtain Spain’s Digital Nomad residence permit in 2026?

Prove qualifying remote employment or business outside Spain, qualifications, sufficient income, social security and the remaining requirements, then file a coherent application through the appropriate route.

Can I apply while in Spain on a tourist visa issued by another Schengen country?

An in-country application is generally possible during lawful stay. The basis and date of entry, remaining permitted days and any violations must be checked; a valid Schengen visa alone does not remove those checks.

How long is the permit granted for?

An in-country residence authorisation is generally granted for up to three years, but not beyond the documented employment or professional relationship. The consular visa route follows a different sequence.

Can I qualify without a university degree?

Yes. You may show a qualification from a recognised university, vocational training institution or business school, or at least three years of relevant documented professional experience.

Can an owner of their own company apply?

Yes, but ownership, genuine business activity, the remote nature of the work, the applicant’s relationship with the company, income and social-security arrangements must all be evidenced.

Can I include my spouse and children?

Yes. A spouse or registered partner, minor children and, where the law allows, other dependent family members may apply together or join later with proof of relationship and sufficient means.

How much income does a family need?

For 2026, the main applicant must show €34 188 per year — 200% of the SMI; the first family member €12 820.50 — 75%; and each additional member €4 273.50 — 25%. Thresholds change with the SMI.

What happens after a positive decision?

Review the conditions and validity, register for the TIE, comply with applicable registration, tax and social-security duties, preserve evidence of continuing activity and track the renewal deadline.

Can I work with Spanish clients?

Limited Spanish client work may be possible for professional activity while the main activity remains abroad. The permitted share, contract model and tax treatment require individual assessment; employee rules differ.
