Foreign employer

Coherent evidence

03 · Strategy

One evidential narrative

We connected corporate records, remote-work permission, experience, income and social security.

Evidence matrix

Employment relationship

Agreement, employer letter, permission to work from Spain and company records.

Professional experience

Certificates and records demonstrating relevant qualifications and actual duties.

Income and banking

Payslips, statements and consistency between payments and the agreement.

Social security

The applicable mechanism for proving coverage was determined and documented separately.
